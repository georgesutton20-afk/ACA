# ACA Exam Reference (research-verified, June 2026)

Source-verified reference used to author exam-accurate mock questions. Figures
cross-checked against ICAEW + GOV.UK/HMRC + reputable tuition providers
(Kaplan, BPP, First Intuition, ACA Simplified). Questions in this app are
**original** and replicate ICAEW exam *format, mark allocation and difficulty*
— they are not copied from copyrighted past papers.

> **Syllabus transition:** ICAEW is rolling out "Next Generation ACA" (Certificate
> from Sept 2025, Professional from Mar 2026, Advanced from Jul 2027). Module
> names below are the legacy/2024–25 names. Pass marks & formats are stable.

## Pass marks (corrected)
- **Certificate: 55%** · **Professional: 55%** · **Advanced: 50%** (all three, incl. Case Study).

## Certificate Level — computer-based, on-demand, **closed book**, 55%
Objective testing: MCQ, multiple-response, numeric entry (+ short scenarios on some).

| Exam | Duration | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting | 90 min (Next Gen 120) | 24 objective (60%) + 1–2 long-form (40%) |
| Assurance | 90 min | 50 objective × 2 marks |
| Business, Technology & Finance | 90 min | 50 objective × 2 marks |
| Law | 90 min | 50 objective × 2 marks |
| Management Information | 90 min | ~32 objective (80%) + 1 scenario (20%) |
| Principles of Taxation | 90 min | 40 objective (80%) + 2 tax-computation scenarios (20%); on-screen tax tables |

## Professional Level — computer-based, 55%, 2.5–3h
Scenario-based multi-part questions; mark allocations stated; computation + written;
mark schemes deliberately over-allocated (write 1 point/mark); own-figure follow-through.

| Exam | Duration | Materials | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit & Assurance | 2.5h | Permitted texts | Risk, evidence/procedures, ethics, reporting |
| Financial Accounting & Reporting | 3h | Permitted texts | 4 Qs: single-entity, IFRS explanation, cash flow, consolidation |
| Tax Compliance | 2.5h | Permitted texts | 5 scenario Qs: IT/NIC, CT, CGT/IHT, VAT, ethics |
| Business Planning: Taxation | 2.5h | Open book | Tax planning in business scenarios |
| Financial Management | 2.5h | Closed book | Financing / hedging / investment-valuation |
| Business Strategy & Technology | 2.5h | Closed book | 3 essay-scenario Qs; ~30% knowledge |

## Advanced Level — computer-based, **open book**, 50%
Long-form integrated; marked on technical content + four professional skills
(Assimilating & Using Information; Structuring Problems & Solutions; Applying
Judgement; Concluding, Recommending & Communicating).

- **Corporate Reporting** — 3.5h, 3 questions (Q1 ~40-mark mini-case in an audit context).
- **Strategic Business Management** — 3.5h, 2 questions (per-requirement marks withheld).
- **Case Study** — 4h; exec summary + 3 equally-weighted requirements; marked over a
  ~240-point grid; must reach 50% in each requirement.

## Examiner themes to replicate
Application over recall; reference the scenario's specific facts/figures; ~1.5 min/mark;
own-figure credit in computations; judgement & recommendations score highly at Advanced.

## EXAMINABLE UK tax figures — **Finance Act 2024 / 2024-25** (ACA 2026 sittings)
> ACA 2026 tax sittings (TC, PoT, BPT) examine **FA 2024 (2024/25)**. Use these for
> tax questions. Live 2025/26–2026/27 figures differ and are for context only.

**Income tax:** PA £12,570 (taper £1/£2 over £100,000, nil at £125,140); 20% on first
£37,700; 40% to £125,140; 45% above. Dividend allowance £500; dividend rates
8.75% / 33.75% / 39.35%. PSA £1,000 / £500 / £0; savings starting-rate band £5,000 @ 0%.

**NIC (2024/25):** Employee Class 1 main 8% (£12,570–£50,270), 2% above. Employer
(secondary) 13.8%, secondary threshold £9,100. Employment allowance £5,000. Class 2:
no liability from 2024/25 (treated as paid if profits ≥ £6,725). Class 4 6%
(£12,570–£50,270) + 2% above.

**CGT:** Annual exempt amount £3,000. **BADR 10%** (lifetime limit £1,000,000).
Main rates: where a rate is needed, **state it in the question** (the 30 Oct 2024
change to 18%/24% straddles the FA boundary — avoid relying on an unstated main rate).

**Corporation tax:** Main rate 25% (>£250,000); small profits 19% (<£50,000); marginal
relief between, fraction **3/200**. AIA £1,000,000; full expensing 100% (main pool).

**VAT:** Standard 20%, reduced 5%, zero 0%. Registration £90,000; deregistration £88,000.

**IHT:** Nil-rate band £325,000; residence nil-rate band £175,000 (taper £1/£2 over £2m);
death rate 40%; reduced charity rate 36% (≥10% of net estate to charity).

### Live-rate divergences (real world, NOT examinable in 2026)
- CGT main rates 18%/24% (from 30 Oct 2024); BADR 14% (2025/26) → 18% (from 6 Apr 2026).
- Employer NIC 15%, secondary threshold £5,000 (from 6 Apr 2025).
- Dividend ordinary/upper rates 10.75%/35.75% from 2026/27.
